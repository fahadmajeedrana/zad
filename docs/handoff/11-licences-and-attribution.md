## Arramooz root lexicon: GPL

- `ROOTS_RAW` (in `src/roots.js` and `data/core.json`) is derived from **Arramooz**, an Arabic dictionary for morphological analysis by Taha Zerrouki et al.: https://github.com/linuxscout/arramooz. It is licensed under the GPL.
- The repository was set up as **GPL-3.0** for this reason, and `dist/NOTICE.md` carries the attribution.
- The app shows a credit on the Maker screen, with a link. **Keep that credit, and keep the NOTICE file** in the rebuilt app.
- If you ever want a non-GPL licence for the app code, the lexicon would have to be replaced or kept as a clearly separate data file with its own licence. Get advice before doing either.
